Case studies

Paths in progress

Each case follows one real build: the people it serves, the person building it, and the AI doing much of the work. Desire coding names what happens when the people with the need can lay their own gravel. These cases record where those paths go.

Every case runs two streams. Field notes tell the story for anyone, technical or not. Lab notes are for people who build or study AI systems, and they lean on open questions. Posts appear when a pattern emerges, grouped by theme instead of by date.
